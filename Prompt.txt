ENGINE:

UI FEATURES:

- Toggle for background removal on/off.
- "Generate" button, status line, 3D preview of the result (Gradio Model3D), and a Download button for the .glb.
- Every generated model is saved to an `output/` folder with a timestamped filename, and a simple history list in the UI lets me re-preview or re-download earlier results.
